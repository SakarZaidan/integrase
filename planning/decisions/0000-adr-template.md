# NNNN. Short title stating the decision

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Amends:** `subplans/NN-name.md`, if this changes a subplan's conclusion
**Deciders:** who made the call

## Context

What situation forces a decision now. Include the evidence: a measurement, a failed assumption, a
new dependency, a user report. If the context is "we never decided this properly," say that.

Keep this factual. The argument goes in the next section.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated in one or two sentences, in the present tense. "We depend on X." Not "we
should probably consider depending on X."

## Consequences

What becomes true because of this, including the bad parts. An ADR listing only benefits is a sales
document, not a record.

- What gets easier
- What gets harder
- What this commits us to
- What it would cost to reverse

## Verification

How we will know this was right, or wrong. A benchmark, a test, a metric, or an honest "we will find
out when someone tries to use it."
